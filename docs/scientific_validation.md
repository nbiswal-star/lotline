# Scientific validation: what the evidence supports

**Scope.** This is retrospective internal validation of a decision-support method on one dated Pittsburgh Treasurer Sale snapshot. The evaluated construct is conformance to LotLine's declared screening policy: source reconciliation, selective abstention under conflict or missingness, bounded score uncertainty, and explicit human routing. It is not total development feasibility, legal or zoning ground truth, financial viability, predictive performance, or external validation.

Reproduce the complete artifact with:

```bash
uv run python -m evaluation.run
```

The command writes the parcel-level tables, denominators, exceptions, assertions, hashes and limitations to `docs/validation/results.md` and `docs/validation/results.json`.

## AI-native claim and no-AI counterfactual

The AI contribution is not the parcel decision. It is semantic retrieval from changing, heterogeneous public-record prose. Claude proposes passages about physical condition, demolition, permits, condemnation and enforcement; deterministic code checks record identity, field, recorded date, exact quote, quote boundaries, clipped negation, instruction-like text and permit references, and neutrally notes when later-dated records exist. It does not establish temporal truth. The current live reader unions proposals from three same-model runs, reports the proposal frequency of each quote, and applies a same-model entailment check before a semantic label can be displayed. A quote may be included after one run if it passes every verifier; recurrence is evidence shown to the analyst, not an inclusion threshold or independent confirmation. Code may attach the exact loaded `Active` status from an AI-surfaced condemned record as deterministic enrichment.

The primary AI-delta evidence is a balanced development sample of 150 parcels randomly sampled within two eligible Pittsburgh proxy strata: 75 with a qualifying completed demolition permit and 75 with an active condemned-list entry and no demolition permit. The second stratum is a City-record proxy, not verified present-day standing structures. For the frozen `k=1`, no-entailment-judge configuration, the primary AI rule returned 30/30 precision against that proxy (95% Wilson interval 88.6–100%) and 30/75 recall (29.7–51.3%), with **0/75 proxy-discordant demolition assertions**. The highest-F1 pre-call simple rule (B4) returned 46/61 precision (75.4%; 63.3–84.5%) and 46/75 recall (50.0–71.5%), with **15/75 proxy-discordant assertions**. A stronger post-hoc contextual deterministic rule (B5) returned 35/37 precision, 35/75 recall, 2/75 proxy discordances and F1 .625, versus AI F1 .571. AI therefore did not win on recall or F1; it demonstrated a conservative precision edge, not necessity or overall superiority. The full protocol, baselines, cost, latency, error analysis and confidence intervals are in `docs/validation/ai_scale_results.md`.

The earlier 21-record, 3-parcel audit remains only a development conformance check; it did not reproduce under a later live run and is not a headline result. The 150-parcel result is also not a benchmark: sampling is balanced, the reference encodes a derived City-record proxy rather than present-day site truth, only one extraction pass was run, and the system and analysis were developed on this sample. The current three-run-plus-judge live configuration has not been evaluated at this scale. The sampled derived reference and full model inputs are committed, but the complete upstream permit/condemned rows used to construct the candidate frame are not; a live refetch is mutable and cannot independently reconstruct the original frame. The study does not establish model necessity, external validity, analyst time saved or superior decision quality. The engine outcome is identical with or without AI; the measured delta is fewer proxy-discordant semantic assertions relative to specific simple rules while presenting exact dated evidence to a human resolver.

A post hoc contextual deterministic rule that rejects negated, planned and repair-or-demolish wording reached 35/37 precision and 35/75 recall (F1 0.625): stronger than the primary AI on recall and F1, with 2 rather than 0 proxy discordances. A paired stratified parcel bootstrap against the strongest-F1 simple rule estimates an AI precision delta of +0.246 [0.157, 0.338] and recall delta of −0.213 [−0.347, −0.080]. A counterfactual replay disabling deterministic verifier layers one at a time did not change parcel-level demolition confusion counts on these cached proposals, although it changed which evidence items survived. These findings narrow the claim: this study supports a precision–coverage tradeoff, not LLM necessity or overall superiority.

The committed calibration artifact estimates a parcel-level target for this frozen development experiment. It is deliberately incompatible with the runtime item-confidence schema and is not displayed as item confidence; reusing it would change the target and overstate validation.

### Full-cohort multimodal parcel audit (review-signal study, not visual accuracy)

All 96 sale-feed parcels have source-cached Esri World Imagery with the target Allegheny County parcel polygon overlaid. Claude Opus 5 returns an enum-only observation for a structure-like footprint, surface cover, street context and image quality; arbitrary model prose is not accepted. Cached outputs load only when both image and complete prompt/schema contract hashes match. Section 11 of the generated validation report reproduces the current cohort audit: 96/96 valid images and reads, 3 `clearly_visible`, 48 `not_visible`, and 45 `unclear`. Comparing against the dated assessment class produced 36 structure-classified/no-visible-footprint human-review flags, including 29 among records the engine routed as structures. Across two consecutive runs, exact categories agreed on 93/96 parcels, total flags varied 35–36, abstentions varied 44–45, and the same 29 structure-routed records were flagged. The records-only counterfactual produces no visual–record signal. This is the multimodal delta: a new, prioritized discrepancy queue, not a changed parcel decision. The first raw cache was overwritten, so the two-run result is a build-time stability record rather than a preregistered reliability estimate.

The assessment class is not an image label or ground truth, no blind present-condition labels exist, and the imagery acquisition date is not provided. A non-visible footprint is not proof of demolition or vacancy; `unclear` is an explicit abstention. Benezet and Michigan returned `not_visible`; Centre returned `unclear`, so the demo's critical records conflict remains unresolved. The audit establishes working full-cohort coverage and bounded failure behavior—not visual accuracy, current condition, improved decision quality, or LLM superiority.

The tested contrast is records-only versus a zero-shot multimodal proof of concept. The deterministic record engine performs no pixel analysis; Claude supplies bounded image categories without task-specific labels, and deterministic code enforces their vocabulary and consequences. Handcrafted computer vision, open building-footprint overlays, classical segmentation and task-specific detectors remain unevaluated alternatives. This study does not establish that learned vision—or an LLM—is necessary or superior. A prospectively labeled comparison against those alternatives at matched abstention and coverage is the correct next experiment.

## Scripted live-model smoke run

`uv run python scripts/run_live_ai_smoke.py` records status/frame metadata—never model prose or credentials—to `docs/validation/live_ai_smoke.json`. On the recorded 2026-09-27 run with `claude-opus-5`, 12/12 scripted demo question–parcel attempts were answered or safely declined, and 5/5 adversarial or out-of-scope attempts were rejected or declined. This is a scripted smoke run, not a frozen benchmark or validation result. The same artifact records 6/8 usable cached ZBA cards; the two failed extractions remain unavailable, and the convenience set is neither an approval-rate sample nor a prediction base.

## AI safety findings that changed the implementation

- Exact quotation proves provenance, not meaning. A discovered false accept allowed a neutral exact quote to inherit a demolition label. The verifier now assigns `unverified_label` whenever deterministic semantic support is absent or disagrees, and derives relevance independently.
- Same-record overlap overstated stability. The live reader now unions exact semantic tuples across three proposal runs, reports tuple recurrence rather than requiring it, and applies a same-model entailment check; the UI/docs do not call those runs independent agreement.
- ZBA relief enums did not prove relief meaning. Relief kinds are now checked against scoped deterministic patterns. A dedicated regression test rejects a dimensional-setback decision mislabeled as a use variance.
- ZBA precedent extraction remains secondary. Eight hand-selected decisions cannot support an approval rate or prediction claim; cards are examples, not forecasts.

## Threats and smallest defensible next study

The strongest remaining threat is selection and labeling bias. Before the next sale, freeze prompts, verifier, strong budget-matched baselines and output limits; then have two practitioners label entire parcels blind to model output. Compare a structured/recency parser, BM25 or TF-IDF retrieval, unconstrained extraction as a safety ablation, and the verified reader. Report record- and event-level TP/FP/FN, critical-conflict-pair recall, exact-tuple stability, wrong accepted outputs conditional on acceptance, refusal/error states, tokens, latency and dated cost. Split by parcel, never by fields from the same record. Report every disagreement and raw numerator/denominator; do not claim general accuracy from the current sample.

For Ask LotLine, freeze at least 30 independently written question–parcel pairs spanning paraphrases, misspellings, multi-intent, underspecified and out-of-scope questions, plus 30 adversarial questions. Compare with a deterministic router and BM25 retrieval over the same approved claim/excerpt corpus. Report wrong accepted answers over accepted answers, not only over all attempts.

## Reproducibility and claim discipline

The engine and evaluation are deterministic in process, with byte-identical serialized results across two runs and a recorded result hash. `uv.lock` is committed. Application code is mechanically barred from test labels and golden-set answer columns. The app works without a key; verified caches are rechecked against current source text before display. Upstream public-source extraction is not reproducible from this repository and is stated as such. All findings remain limited to the dated snapshot and must be refreshed and revalidated for another sale.
