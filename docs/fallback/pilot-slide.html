From hackathon build to one-sale-cycle pilot

Hypothetical operating plan · no agency participation or affiliation is implied

Proposed operating owner

  • One CDC or land-bank acquisitions analyst owns the screening queue.
  • A data steward manually refreshes sources when the City advertisement is published.
  • Planning, PLI, Treasurer, County Real Estate and licensed specialists resolve named checks.
  • Changed universe counts stop the import for review.

Measure the pilot

  • Hours from advertisement to review-ready shortlist.
  • Decision-changing conflicts caught before paid diligence.
  • Packets accepted without a missing owner or next check.
  • Inputs: staff time, optional hosting and optional Claude API use.
Limits stay visible: title, legal access, utilities, survey, appraisal and market demand are not established.
